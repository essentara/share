Hot Buttered Epiphanies · Season One

Hot Buttered Epiphanies is looking for a podcast manager.

The show

Hot Buttered Epiphanies is an advice-column (Dear Abby-style) podcast from Dr. Lissa Rankin (NYT bestselling author) and Axel Mansoor (Clubhouse Icon, musician, and facilitator).

Listeners send in letters. We answer them through an Internal Family Systems lens, with real care and a healthy amount of laughing at ourselves. Every episode ends with an original musical mantra from Axel that responds to the letter.

  1. Season one starts fall 2026: twelve weekly episodes across twelve weeks.
  2. Recorded in batches, three or four sessions for the whole season.
  3. Conversations run 90 minutes and land at 60 to 75. Light edit, not a tight cut.
  4. The pilot is already recorded, edited, and ready to go.
  5. Distribution runs on Substack, Apple, and Spotify.

You're not starting from zero

  • Lissa brings a New York Times bestselling author's platform: an established Substack with a paid subscriber base in the hundreds and a much larger free list, plus deep roots in the IFS world.
  • Axel brings an Instagram audience of about 20K, plus the Lullaby Club community: the nightly Clubhouse singing room that grew into an Amazon Original show, and an active mailing list.
  • Both of us promote to our own audiences from day one.

The role

You run the show end to end so the two of us can stay talent. You own whether it ships on its date and whether it sounds right. Audience growth is a separate lane we will add later, not something we are putting on you.

Scope

About 130 hours across twelve weeks, or 10 to 11 a week. Batching the recordings keeps scheduling from scaling with it.
