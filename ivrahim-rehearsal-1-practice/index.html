Hine Ma Tov

Working form

  1. Sparse opening. Axel and Nick begin. Dylan enters after the first round and builds in stages.
  2. Head / chorus. Keep the groove restrained until the hats arrive.
  3. Verse 1. Pocket first. Bass anchors the room.
  4. Post-chorus / breakdown. Keep the sixteenth-note hats moving.
  5. Verse 2. Return without adding density too early.
  6. Speaking vamp. Hold a simple groove under Axel.
  7. Congregational melody. All three sing the familiar “Hine ma tov u'ma na'im” melody in unison or harmony.
  8. “September” bridge. Slow on the arrival chord. Nick gets four bars of pocket before embellishing. Short verse only, then accelerate back.
  9. Final full chorus. Biggest vocal stack. End immediately after the full form with the four-count cue and final hit.

Dylan

Build the opening kick, then snare, then hats, then full groove at the post. Keep sixteenths through the breakdown. Open-and-close disco hats are part of the sound.

Nick

Groove first, embellishment later. Try the high octave or falsetto on the head. Learn one harmony for the congregational melody.

Song 2 · 6:26

Adon Olam

Full rehearsal run

Download audio

Working form

  1. Dramatic opening. Rubato guitar and vocal. Dylan gives “John Bonham at 10%.” Bigger and more cinematic.
  2. Compression into groove. Four-on-the-floor and bass together for two measures, like a heartbeat after the opening.
  3. A section / verse. Bass line is the heart. Guitar joins the instrumental groove, then can drop out under the vocal.
  4. Build and big return. Expand into the full, high-impact section.
  5. Compressed groove reset. Drums and bass reset before the next vocal entrance.
  6. B section / lai-dai singalong. Crowd-facing chant. Guitar has one extra turnaround. Drums carry the motion while bass and harmony shift underneath.
  7. Ending. Drum-groove change twice. Final repetition lands a cappella.

Lock at rehearsal: play each section once while saying the chord names and bar counts into the recorder. The rehearsal audio is the reference until that chart exists.

Dylan

Make the opening dramatic without going full volume. Own the two-measure compression, carry the B-section motion, and lock the two groove changes into the a cappella ending.

Nick

Treat the bass line as the heart of the A section. Enter the compressed groove with Dylan, keep the resets simple, and learn the harmony movement under the lai-dai section from the recording.
