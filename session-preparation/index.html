Preparing for our session

A few practical things to have together before we meet :)

  • Wear something comfortable that you can move and settle in.
  • Please avoid caffeine and solid foods for the three hours before our session. Smoothies are okay.
  • Leave a little breathing room afterward, if you can.
  • Review the preparation notes we’ve agreed on. If anything is unclear or has changed, let me know through our agreed private contact route.

Before we meet, please complete the Session intake form (including the agreement and waiver) and the music questionnaire. If you’ve already completed either form, no need to fill it out again.

Your appointment email has your time and arrival details. Rescheduling requires at least 48 hours’ notice.
