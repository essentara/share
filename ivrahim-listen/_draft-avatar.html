An Altar Ego

IVRAHIM

He's the avatar I become in service of a calling: to help people remember the ancient and mysterious.

The name

I am from everywhere and nowhere: I am Ashkenazi, Mauritian-Creole, and Indian; I come from a family of Jews, Muslims, and Catholics. Avraham and Ibrahim are my given names. My ancestors feared each other and the violence continues still. Through music and creativity, there's an opportunity to join them together. IVRAHIM is the name I use to bridge my ancestries and create from all of them.

The look

Bone, char, and copper. Traditional, experimental, and sensual in the same frame.

Axel and Ivrahim

Axel Mansoor is the private self and the builder. Ivrahim is the project, and it gets built in public.

That means I can’t fold my old catalogue into this. Nothing carries over. Clean slate, brand new, born.

It also means the doubt you’ll see is mine, not his. I’ll tell you when I don’t know what I’m doing. Ivrahim never will.
