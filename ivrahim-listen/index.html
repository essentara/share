The name

I am from everywhere and nowhere: I am Ashkenazi, Mauritian-Creole, and Indian; I come from a family of Jews, Muslims, and Catholics.

Avraham and Ibrahim are my given names. My ancestors feared each other and their legacy of conflict endures today.

IVRAHIM is the name I received to heal that legacy in the name of harmony: weaving my ancestry through music and creativity.
